Istanbul → Astana

A taste of Türkiye

Istanbul Restaurant brings Turkish cooking to Astana. Its profile introduces a chef from Istanbul and changing Turkish specials.

Visitor features · Visual identity

The restaurant

More than one familiar dish

Guests can find Turkish breakfast, döner, kebabs and künefe alongside café dishes. Rotating specials keep the selection changing.

The Uly Dala location combines a dining room with a decorated terrace. The flame logo, warm lights and red flowers form a clear identity that continues from the street into the interior.
