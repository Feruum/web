Frequently asked questions

Where is the restaurant?

Uly Dala Avenue 56, Esil district, Astana. The Visit page includes a map.

When is it open?

The public listing shows daily opening hours from 08:00 until 02:00.

Do I need to book?

Booking is available. Call +7 702 100 62 88 for a real reservation.

What food is served?

Turkish breakfast, döner, kebabs, künefe and a broad café menu. See Menu.

Is it suitable for families?

Yes. The listing includes a children’s menu and a children’s corner.

Is takeaway available?

Yes. Dine-in, takeaway and delivery are included in the listed services.
